# Accessibility

- Semantic landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), lang="en"
- Every form control has a label or aria-label
- Feedback announced with aria-live="polite"
- Keyboard navigation, visible :focus-visible outline, skip link
- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion
- Responsive layout, works at 200% zoom
- Meaningful alt text on images
